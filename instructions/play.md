The weather changes by itself each dawn, and on a variable day again at 14:00: advance and travel tell it, weather tells it again. Tell it as the sky the characters see and feel, never its score, and let it weigh on travel, camps and fights. A place as hot as a desert, or as cold as the poles or a high pass: write climate: torrid or climate: polar in its environment (the biomes desert and snow already count). When the story changes the weather (a spell, a storm you decide), weather-set, on the scale weather gives.
